# Personal Expense Tracker — Application Summary

A mobile application for **Android and iPhone** that helps individuals record personal expenses and understand their spending.

## Version 1

- **Manual expense entry:** Record expenses directly in the app.
- **Daily, weekly, and monthly history:** Browse spending across days, weeks, and months.
- **Customizable categories:** Start with built-in expense categories and customize them to suit personal needs.
- **PKR only:** Track all expenses in Pakistani rupees.
- **Device-only storage:** Keep expense data on the phone, with no account or cloud synchronization.

## Scope Limits

Version 1 does not include income tracking, receipt scanning, bank integration, multiple currencies, or manual backup and restore. Without a recovery feature, losing the phone or deleting app data may permanently lose expense history.

## Future Direction

Income tracking may be added in a later version. The technology stack and visual design have not yet been approved.
